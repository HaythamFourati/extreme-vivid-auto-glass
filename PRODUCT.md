# PRODUCT.md — Extreme Vivid Auto Glass

## What the business actually does
Mobile auto glass company in the Baltimore, MD area. They drive to the customer
(home, work, fleet lot) and repair or replace vehicle glass. Operating since 2003.

## Who visits the site (and their mindset)
Almost nobody browses auto glass for fun. Visitors arrive **mid-problem**:
- "There's a crack spreading across my windshield."
- "Someone broke into my car, the side window is gone."
- "A rock chipped my glass, will it spread?"
- "Dealer says I need ADAS recalibration after a windshield job."
- Fleet/commercial manager with a vehicle down.

State of mind: urgent, slightly stressed, often on a phone, wants to know FAST:
1. Can they fix MY problem? 2. Will they come to ME? 3. How do I start, right now?

## The one job of the homepage
Convert an anxious problem-haver into a **phone call or quote submission** in under
a minute. Everything else is secondary. Phone number and Quote CTA must be reachable
above the fold on every device.

## Conversion principles applied
- Phone + Quote in the sticky header and a mobile bottom bar (Call · Quote · Services).
